Hi, I’m Arnav.

AV-01 · SIH 2026

Prototype

ODYSSEY

Train ETA and delay propagation for Indian Railways

One late train makes the next one late, and the one after that. ODYSSEY predicts arrival times across the network and shows how a single delay ripples down a corridor. Section-level XGBoost and LightGBM models feed a spatio-temporal graph neural network.

Co-built with my team for Smart India Hackathon 2026

  • Python

AV-02 · Satellite data

Prototype · demo live

IgnisAlert

Wildfire alerts for villages in the hills of Uttarakhand

It doesn’t detect fires. NASA’s satellites already do that. IgnisAlert takes VIIRS hotspots from NASA FIRMS, groups them into fire events, works out which villages sit uphill of each one, and sends short Hindi SMS and voice calls to the sarpanch, ASHA worker and forest guard. Their replies decide whether an alert escalates.

Pilot districts: Nainital and Pauri Garhwal

  • Python
  • NASA FIRMS
  • VIIRS
  • Geospatial

AV-03 · RegTech

Live

BrandGuard

Compliance checks before anything goes public

Marketing teams publish fast and fix later. BrandGuard checks copy against brand rules, facts and Indian advertising regulation (ASCI rulings and the CCPA framework) before it ships. Every flag cites the rule it broke and suggests a fix. The AI suggests; a person signs off.

Product scoping, regulatory research, build

AV-04 · Civic tech

Live

ReeSolve

Civic complaints that actually get closed

Routes citizen complaints to the right department, merges duplicates and links problems that belong together, like a burst pipe and the pothole it caused. If a deadline slips, or the evidence doesn’t match the closure, the case reopens itself and escalates.
